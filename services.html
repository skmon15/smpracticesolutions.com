Core Services

Focused support in the areas that most directly affect compliance and revenue performance

Each engagement is customized to the organization’s workflow, specialty mix, documentation patterns, and risk profile.

01

Revenue Integrity & Compliance Risk Mitigation

Identify and address risk drivers across professional fee coding, documentation, workflow design, and operational processes before they turn into repeat denials, audit findings, or revenue loss.

  • Professional fee compliance reviews
  • Documentation risk assessment
  • Audit readiness support
  • Policy and workflow alignment
  • Operational risk identification
02

Denials Reduction & Root-Cause Analysis

Reduce preventable denials by identifying whether the real issue is coding variation, documentation gaps, payer-specific rules, charge capture defects, or workflow breakdowns.

  • Denial trend analysis
  • Preventable vs. non-preventable classification
  • Root-cause review by category
  • Corrective action plan development
  • Education-based prevention strategies
03

Provider & Coder Education

Deliver practical education designed to change behavior, improve consistency, and support long-term performance across providers, coders, and leadership teams.

  • Provider documentation improvement
  • Coder accuracy and consistency training
  • Specialty-specific education
  • Audit-driven remediation planning
  • Workflow-based coaching support

Workflow Optimization
